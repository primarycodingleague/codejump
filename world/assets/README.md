# 3D World models

The characters (`characters/`), objects (`objects/`) and the pictures in `thumbs/` (shrunk to 72 px) are made from
**Kenney's** free game assets, www.kenney.nl, released under **Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)**: they are in the
public domain and may be used, changed and shared for any purpose, with no permission or credit needed. We credit
Kenney anyway, with thanks.

Packs used: Mini Characters, Platformer Kit, Nature Kit, Food Kit, Fantasy Town Kit 2.0, City Kit (Suburban), Furniture
Kit and Mini Arena.

What we changed (tools/world-assets/ in this repo does it):

- every file has its texture packed inside it (one `.glb` each) and is scaled to a set height;
- each character's colour map has every part (hair, skin, eyes, top, shorts, shoes) in its own place, so the 3D World
  can recolour one part at a time; one character's toy blaster was taken out;
- the characters' animations are their own (built into each file); the 3D World makes a few more itself (wave, clap,
  cheer, dances, flip, swim, climb…).
