# 3D World models and animations

The characters (`characters/`), objects (`objects/`), animations (`animations/`) and the pictures in `thumbs/`
(shrunk to 72 px) come from **Flock XR** by Flip Computing Ltd, https://github.com/flipcomputing/flock, which is
released under the MIT licence below. They are used unchanged apart from the thumbnails. "Flock XR" and the Flock XR
bird are trademarks of Flip Computing Ltd and are not used in CodeJump (the Flock bird model is left out on purpose).

**Before 3D World goes public, check with Flip Computing:** Flock's docs say its characters were rigged with Mixamo's
auto-rigger and that the animations are made for that rig. If any of the animation clips came from Mixamo's library,
Adobe's terms don't allow redistributing them as standalone files, so confirm their source (and that they're happy for
CodeJump to use the assets) before `THREED_ON` is switched on.
